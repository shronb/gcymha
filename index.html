WHO WE ARE

Mental health education designed with young people in mind.

GCYMHA is a youth-centered mental health organization created to make psychological knowledge more accessible and to help young people become advocates, researchers, volunteers, and community leaders.

We aim to reduce stigma surrounding mental health, expand access to trustworthy information, and create meaningful opportunities for students interested in psychology and mental healthcare.

Our mission Education • Community • Advocacy

OUR MISSION

Helping the next generation understand mental health earlier.

Mental health should not feel inaccessible because of age, cultural stigma, language, location, or a lack of opportunities.

Our programs introduce young people to psychological science while creating spaces where they can discuss wellbeing, learn from professionals, support their communities, and explore future academic or career paths.

YOUTH IMPACT

Learn. Create. Lead.

Our Mental Health for Youth initiative helps students move beyond simply learning about psychology. Participants can create projects, support communities, explore research, and develop leadership experience.
