IN PERSON CLAREMONT, CALIFORNIA

Peer Support & Art-Based Self-Exploration

In collaboration with Chen Ming, a licensed therapist in China with graduate training at Claremont Graduate University, GCYMHA organized a recurring small-group workshop centered on reflection, emotional expression, and peer connection.

Participants used OH Cards as prompts for personal reflection and group discussion, followed by artistic activities encouraging self-exploration.

Event Recap

  • Ming opened the group with brief introductions.
  • Participants completed two rounds of OH Card activities.
  • Discussions explored self-understanding, unmet needs, personal conflicts, and intuitive interpretations of art.
  • The group concluded by creating drawings representing emotions on traditional Chinese Xuan paper.
