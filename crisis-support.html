FINDING PROFESSIONAL CARE

Therapy & psychiatry

If you have health insurance, your insurer may maintain its own behavioral health referral system or provider network.

Contact your health plan directly for current information about covered therapists, psychiatrists, referrals, copayments, telehealth, and in-person care.

GCYMHA does not provide clinical referrals or guarantee the availability, quality, affordability, or outcome of services offered by third-party providers.
