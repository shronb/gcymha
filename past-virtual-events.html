VIRTUAL CHINESE-LANGUAGE WORKSHOP

新一代内卷：不必要的压力

“Neijuan,” often described as involution or a social “rat race,” refers to increasingly intense competition that can place significant pressure on young people.

This workshop explored how social expectations, conformity, all-or-nothing thinking, and learned helplessness can influence psychological wellbeing.

Event Recap

  • Host Nica led an ACT values activity examining differences between personal values and broader social expectations.
  • Speaker Yixuan discussed mechanisms associated with “Neijuan,” including reduced creativity and pressure to follow social trends.
  • Participants discussed all-or-nothing thinking and learned helplessness, including ideas such as 躺平 and 摆烂.
  • Yixuan introduced strategies for adjusting everyday habits and increasing stress tolerance.
VIRTUAL PEER SUPPORT PANEL

Homeschool / Remote: 青少年长期独处与心理健康

This peer-support discussion explored the psychological challenges that can accompany prolonged isolation, independent study, uncertainty, and changes in social connection.

The conversation focused particularly on the experiences of homeschooled students in China and the unique circumstances they may encounter.

Event Recap

Lin and Otter, using nicknames, facilitated a discussion based on their experiences as homeschooled students in China.

  • Managing prolonged periods of solitude.
  • Maintaining social connection outside a traditional school environment.
  • Navigating uncertainty and independent learning.
  • Building and maintaining healthy relationships.
